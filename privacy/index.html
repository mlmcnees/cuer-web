Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Short version: Cuer does not collect personal information on its own. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Your dance library, music files, and cue cards stay on your own device, in a location you choose.

The one exception is optional and always your choice: if something goes wrong, you can send a problem report (log files and diagnostics) to the developer. Nothing is sent unless you review it and click Send. See Problem Reports below.

What Cuer Is

Cuer is a Windows desktop application for managing round dance music and choreography. It is developed and published by Mike McNees.

Data We Collect

Cuer does not collect any personal information in the background. Specifically, Cuer:

  • Does not require an account, sign-in, or registration of any kind.
  • Does not use analytics, telemetry, or any third-party tracking service, and never sends crash or error reports automatically. A problem report is sent only if you choose to send one (see Problem Reports).
  • Does not display advertising.
  • Does not send your dance library, music files, cue cards, or any other content to the developer or any third party. The only exception is a problem report that you choose to send, which contains the log files and diagnostics described below.

Problem Reports (Optional)

If something goes wrong, Cuer can send a problem report to the developer so the problem can be fixed. This only ever happens when you choose it:

  • You start a report yourself from Settings → General → Report a Problem, or Cuer offers to send one the next time it starts after it did not close properly (for example, after a crash). In both cases a dialog appears first, and nothing is sent unless you click Send. If you click Cancel or Not now, nothing is sent.
  • The dialog shows you the diagnostic details that will be included and lets you add an optional description of what happened. Please don’t put personal or sensitive information in the description.

What a report contains:

  • Cuer’s log files from your most recent sessions (up to five). These record what the app was doing and any errors, and can include folder and file paths on your computer and the names of dances, songs, or venues that appear in error messages. Cuer does not record your computer name or username in its logs, and before a report is sent it replaces your Windows user folder (which contains your username) with %USERPROFILE% in the logs and diagnostics. Anything else in the logs, including other file paths such as a music folder you chose elsewhere, is sent as recorded.
  • A short diagnostics summary: the Cuer version, the Windows version, the name of your active profile, the location and size of your database file, and whether the previous session ended unexpectedly.
  • The description you typed, if any.

A report does not include your database, music files, cue cards, or any other files from your computer.

How it is sent and who handles it: Reports are sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to a small service run by the developer on Microsoft Azure, which forwards each report by email, through the Resend email delivery service, to the developer’s support inbox. To prevent abuse, the service records the sender’s IP address along with a count of reports received per day, and uses that only to limit how many reports can be sent. Microsoft Azure and Resend handle the report only to deliver it.

If you are offline: Cuer saves the report in its data folder and sends it automatically the next time it starts with an internet connection. A report that cannot be sent within 30 days is deleted without being sent.

How it is used: The developer uses reports only to investigate and fix problems with Cuer, and keeps them only as long as needed for that purpose. Reports are never used for advertising or analytics, and are not sold or shared with anyone else.

Where Your Data Lives

All of your data — your dance library, song and choreography details, cue cards, and media files — is stored locally on your own computer, in a local SQLite database and a folder location that you choose. Cuer does not sync this data to any cloud service on its own. If you choose to store your data in a folder that you separately sync with a service like Google Drive or OneDrive, that synchronization is between you and that service, not something Cuer performs or has access to.

Network Use

Apart from the optional problem reports described above, Cuer's network use is minimal and stays on your own device or local network:

  • Remote Control (optional): Cuer can run a small web server on your computer so you can control music playback from another device, such as a phone or tablet, on the same local network (for example, over Wi-Fi at home). This feature is off unless you use it, and no data leaves your local network or reaches the developer.
  • Outbound links: A few places in the app, such as links to release notes or this privacy policy, open a page in your default web browser when you click them. Cuer does not track when or whether you click these.

Microsoft Store & Windows

Cuer is distributed through the Microsoft Store. The Windows operating system and the Microsoft Store platform may independently collect standard diagnostic and usage data as described in Microsoft's Privacy Statement. This collection happens at the operating system and platform level — Cuer's own code does not add to, access, or control it.

Children's Privacy

Cuer is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Problem reports are sent only when a user chooses to send one.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date above.
