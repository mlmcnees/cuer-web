Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

Short version: Cuer does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. Your dance library, music files, and cue cards stay on your own device, in a location you choose.

What Cuer Is

Cuer is a Windows desktop application for managing round dance music and choreography. It is developed and published by Mike McNees.

Data We Collect

Cuer does not collect any personal information. Specifically, Cuer:

  • Does not require an account, sign-in, or registration of any kind.
  • Does not use analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or any third-party tracking service.
  • Does not display advertising.
  • Does not send your dance library, music files, cue cards, or any other content to the developer or any third party.

Where Your Data Lives

All of your data — your dance library, song and choreography details, cue cards, and media files — is stored locally on your own computer, in a local SQLite database and a folder location that you choose. Cuer does not sync this data to any cloud service on its own. If you choose to store your data in a folder that you separately sync with a service like Google Drive or OneDrive, that synchronization is between you and that service, not something Cuer performs or has access to.

Network Use

Cuer's network use is minimal and stays on your own device or local network:

  • Remote Control (optional): Cuer can run a small web server on your computer so you can control music playback from another device, such as a phone or tablet, on the same local network (for example, over Wi-Fi at home). This feature is off unless you use it, and no data leaves your local network or reaches the developer.
  • Outbound links: A few places in the app, such as links to release notes or this privacy policy, open a page in your default web browser when you click them. Cuer does not track when or whether you click these.

Microsoft Store & Windows

Cuer is distributed through the Microsoft Store. The Windows operating system and the Microsoft Store platform may independently collect standard diagnostic and usage data as described in Microsoft's Privacy Statement. This collection happens at the operating system and platform level — Cuer's own code does not add to, access, or control it.

Children's Privacy

Cuer is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to cuer@dancingaround.dance.
